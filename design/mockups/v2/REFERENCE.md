# What the two reference tweets do

Sources, studied frame by frame:
- Divan Raj, x.com/desdivj/status/2117204237868466336: 4 videos (Overview, Reports, Analytics, Projects), invoicing app.
- Saif Shaikh, x.com/saifgeeky/status/2125614177159090260: 7 stills, "luma" credit-card app.

1. **Layout.** One column of white rounded cards (radius ~24px) on a light grey canvas, 16–20px gutters. The header is a large title on the left, a grey subline under it ("Monday, Nov 2"), and two round white icon buttons on the right. Under it: a pill segmented control with a white thumb, then cards. Lists live inside one card, as rows split by hairlines. Saif adds a floating pill tab bar and a pair of half-width tiles.
2. **Type.** A neo-grotesk sans in two or three weights only. Titles ~28–32px semibold with tight tracking. The hero number is ~40–56px medium, with its **decimals or unit set in light grey** ("$248**.9k**", "$86,420**.18**"). Labels are 13px regular grey. Meta is 12px grey with "·" between facts. All numbers are tabular and right-aligned in lists.
3. **Colour.** Nearly monochrome: canvas #EEE, white cards, black ink, two greys. Divan has one signal colour (orange-red) for the main line, "days late" and alerts. Green is for positive chips (a tinted pill, "+18.2%") and success. Primary buttons are solid black pills. Saif adds one brand material (a satin card) and soft tinted tiles, never flat slabs.
4. **Texture and depth.** Cards are flat white with almost no shadow; depth comes from the white card against the grey canvas. Saif's hero card is a *material*: a satin sheen with fine concentric hairline circles and a soft drop shadow. Icon wells are grey circles. Dividers are 1px hairlines inset from the card edge.
5. **Motion.** The segmented thumb slides; numbers re-roll when the period changes; bars and lines redraw. Scrubbing a chart turns the hovered bar solid black and shows a black rounded tooltip (date, bold value, grey note). Bottom sheets slide up over a dimmed screen, and a black button morphs into a green "✓ Reminders sent" pill. Presses scale down slightly.
6. **Numbers and cards.** Each card has a grey label with a delta chip beside it, one big number, and a grey meta line ("$8.0k avg/day · best Oct 15"). Then a three-stat row with coloured dots ("● Invoiced $112.4k"). Charts are grey bars plus one orange line and a dashed green target, with axis labels at the right and a small legend underneath. List rows are an icon circle, a title over grey meta (status in colour), and a right-aligned amount over a small status pill. Saif uses dot-column charts.

## How Hurdle maps onto it
- Par is the "target": a dashed green line. The depositor ("you") is the one coloured line. The curator's strategy is the grey line, and the grey bars show each hour's gap.
- A miss (bond paid) takes the signal orange, like "12 days late". A beat is a green chip.
- The bond works like Saif's balance card: an amount, a progress bar against the 2% floor, and "available" at the edges.
