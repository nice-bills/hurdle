Clear the bar

A curator manages your liquidity and posts a bond. Every hour the pool scores them against par, a plain full-range LP. Beat par, they keep a carry. Miss, the bond pays you the gap.

See the vaults →How par is computed
01 · BOND

The curator posts a bond. It caps how much they can manage.

02 · SCORE

Each hour the vault settles against par, computed onchain. No oracle.

03 · PAY

Above par: 20% carry. Below par: the bond tops you up.

04 · FIRE

Bond under 1% of NAV: the curator is fired and the vault goes passive.
