A curator vault has to beat par, the return of a passive full-range LP that the pool itself computes onchain from its own fee growth and TWAP; if it misses, the curator's bond pays depositors the shortfall, and the curator earns carry (20%) only on outperformance.

Floor: bond below 2% of NAVDeposits and curator rebalances freeze until the curator tops up the bond.
Half floor: bond below 1% of NAVA miss that leaves the bond here fires the curator; the vault parks passive full range.
