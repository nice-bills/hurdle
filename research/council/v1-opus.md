## Why not 10 (blocking items, most important first)
1. Live deployment (README.md:36–55) — The deployment shows setup only. When I read the hook (`oracleState(poolId)`), the ring held 2 observations: the pool init and the swaps of the vault-setup step. Its `lastTimestamp` (1794920097) is 3 s after the vaults started, so no swap has hit the pool since deployment. Both vaults have `start.timestamp` equal to the deploy time, `deficit` 0 and `fired` false. Their first 3600 s epoch had not ended when I read them. A judge on uniscan sees mocks, a pool, two bonds and two deposits, but no settlement, no shortfall payment, no carry and no firing on chain. The live system therefore shows none of the mechanism. To reach a 10: run a scripted on-chain scenario on the live contracts. Feed a compressed real-swap path through the swap router for a few hours, settle every epoch, and drive Chaser through a miss and a bond payout to `Fired` and a park. Then list the tx hashes (Settled, Fired, Rebalanced) in the README. A small keeper that keeps calling `settle()` would also keep the vaults moving while judges look.
2. Demo delivery (IDEA.md:63 and :72 say "video left"; README.md:93 has a localhost-only UI) — There is no video, and no hosted UI. Judges score the demo and the UI, and right now both need a clone plus a Python server. To reach a 10: record a 60–90 s video of the range week (Patient earns carry, Chaser's bond drains, FIRED, depositor ≥ par). Host `demo/` as a static site (GitHub Pages works: the page only fetches JSON) and link both at the top of the README.
3. UI misstates the fire rule (demo/ui/index.html:158, :204, :309–314, :322) — The header says "fire at bond < 2% NAV", and the meter tick and dotted line are drawn at `minBondBps` with the label "fire below …". The banner says "bond exhausted → passive". The contract does something else. It fires only on a settlement that paid a shortfall and left the bond below half the floor (HurdleVault.sol:394, i.e. 1%). Below 2% it only freezes deposits and rebalances. The demo shows the conflict on screen. In the down week, Chaser's bond line dips under the red "fire" line while its card reads ACTIVE. In the range week, Chaser is fired with 0.91% bond left, so the bond was not exhausted. To reach a 10: draw two levels, a 2% freeze floor and a 1% fire-on-miss level. Relabel the banner as "miss left bond < ½ floor → fired, parked passive".
4. Headline numbers disagree between the docs and the UI, down week (IDEA.md:75–79, README via audit2.md:38–45, demo/out/down-week.json) — The docs' tables use token1 units. In the replay, token1 is WETH (audit2.md:45: "valued in token1"). IDEA.md:75 still labels these numbers "values in USD". The UI converts to USD, so a judge reading both sees different results. Patient bond paid is 10.23% in the docs and 8.77% in the UI. Patient NAV vs par is −4.25% and −3.93%. Chaser bond paid is 8.22% and 7.05%. Chaser's bond left is 2.3% and 1.97%. To reach a 10: pick one unit (USD) and regenerate every table from the same `build_json.py` output, so the README, IDEA and UI quote identical numbers.
5. Submission text overclaims and is stale (IDEA.md:3, :24–25, :62–63, :72, :86) — Several claims do not match the code:
   - The text says "the hook computes the passive benchmark itself, every block", "Each block, the hook updates the benchmark" and that "the hook knows the vault's exact positions". In the code, the hook stores only a TWAP accumulator and a block-open tick and fee-growth snapshot. The vault computes par in closed form from two snapshots at settlement (HurdleVault.sol:470).
   - DoD #3 claims "<40k MET: ~12k steady state". README.md:23 and research/phase3-twap.md:110 give 39,914 steady state, and 58,454 under `--isolate`. Real swaps are separate transactions with cold access, so the realistic number is the isolated one, and the target is not met.
   - DoD #4 and workflow step 6 still say "redeploy pending".

   A judge who checks the paste-in text against the code finds mismatches on the core claim. To reach a 10: rewrite the pitch around what is true and stronger, namely a path-independent closed form that needs zero per-swap benchmark work. Report the isolated gas figure honestly, or bring it under 40k (see Nits). Mark the redeploy as done.
6. Deposit liveness (HurdleVault.sol:179) — Deposits work only in the block where `start.timestamp == block.timestamp`. Several calls settle a finished epoch through `_maybeSettle`: `settle()` (permissionless, :346), any post-epoch `withdraw` and any curator `rebalance`. When one of them lands in an earlier block, the deposit window closes for a full epoch. No attacker is needed: a keeper or an ordinary withdrawer does it. A griefer who settles at the first valid block of every epoch blocks all new deposits indefinitely, for one L2 tx per hour. README Limits says only "depositors wait for an epoch end". It does not say the wait can be unbounded, and for a curated-liquidity vault, onboarding capital is the product. I found this by reading the code and did not write a PoC. To reach a 10: add queued deposit requests (ERC-7540 style), escrowed any time and executed pro rata inside `_settle` after the shortfall and carry steps. Otherwise, document the griefing and test that a deposit in the same block as an external settle still works.
7. Par is endogenous to the curator's own liquidity (HurdleVault.sol:470–480, README Limits) — Par credits `L_par · ΔfeeGrowthGlobal`, and fee growth is per unit of all in-range liquidity, including the vault's own concentrated position. That is the right benchmark for a marginal vault. On a thin, curator-dominated pool, the theme's target case, the curator's concentration dilutes fee growth. Par's fee credit is then far below what a full-range LP would have earned there, so the curator collects carry on fees that do not reflect skill. Neither the Limits section nor any test covers this. The replay adds 1e21 of background full-range liquidity (Replay.t.sol:133–137) and never tests a vault that holds a large share of in-range liquidity. This is a design argument, not a demonstrated loss. To reach a 10: add a Limits entry and a test or replay case where the vault holds ≥50% of in-range liquidity, and report how excess and carry move. Optionally, correct the fee credit using the pool's block-open in-range liquidity versus the vault's own `_liquidity`, or cap the vault's share of pool liquidity.

## Strengths (max 5 lines)
- The mechanism is original and on theme: depositors get a guaranteed passive-LP floor, the curator posts a bond and earns carry, and par comes from the pool's own state with no oracle. A curator is fired automatically.
- The closed-form par checks out. I re-ran `par_check.py --twap` on the range week: max diff 0.000 bp over 167 settlements, with 0 epochs at the cap. It is also fuzz-checked against a real v4 full-range position.
- The manipulation defenses are careful. The TWAP is written before the swap, fee growth is read at block open, donations are blocked, rebalance swaps are bounded by spot, block-open price and TWAP, and the rebalance budget satisfies 4·dev ≤ minBond.
- Two independent audits have regression tests mapped to each finding and are checked by mutation. In my own dirs, 94 tests pass and the replay is skipped. The README Limits section is unusually honest.
- The replay is built on real mainnet swaps (five weeks), and the chart UI tells the story in one view on desktop.

## Nits (optional, do not affect score)
- test/audit/Audit2Regression.t.sol:261 `test_F_settleTiming` only logs and cannot fail, yet audit2 #8 cites it as proof. I ran it: the late settle leaves the curator about 2.4e12 wei (~4e-7 of its value) worse off, so the claim holds on this path. Add `assertLe(curB, curA)` and fuzz the path.
- Gas: `_blockOpenFeeGrowth` costs two 256-bit SSTOREs per block. Per-epoch fee-growth deltas fit easily in 128 bits, and wrapping subtraction mod 2^128 is still correct. Storing the low 128 bits of each in one slot saves one SSTORE (~5k cold), which helps item 5.
- The UI overflows horizontally at phone width (390 px): the cards and footer are clipped. `body { overflow:hidden }` with a fixed 330 px card column hides this.
- The scenario dropdown lists the `mock-*` synthetic weeks next to the real ones. Drop them from `scenarios.json` before judging.
- The NatSpec at HurdleHook.sol:14 still says "Phase 3: a stripped-down …", which reads as a work-in-progress note. HurdleHook.sol:64 has a stray blank line.
- I skimmed the deploy scripts (DeployVaults.s.sol) but did not read them line by line. I did not run the replay.
