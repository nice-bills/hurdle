## Why not 10 (blocking items, most important first)
1. Live deployment — no vault on the live dApp can take a deposit. Read-only `eth_call` from the depositor address: Chaser `deposit(...)` reverts `DeficitOutstanding` (0x929b7e84; deficit 6.7% of NAV vs a 1 bp tolerance) and it is also `BondBelowFloor`-frozen (bond 1.49% of NAV, floor 2%, out of range at ticks 196050–196450 vs spot 198250, "not earning"). Patient is fired, bond 0, reverts `DepositWindowClosed` and would hit `CapacityExceeded` next. The "Try it on testnet" button leads to a deposit tab that cannot succeed, so a judge cannot complete the main flow on the shipped vaults. Make it 10: before submission leave at least one healthy, depositable vault (a third fresh one with a bond sized for the scripted drops, or let the keeper top up the Chaser bond and re-center), and keep the fired Patient as the failure showcase. A fresh vault would also show the 5-minute window in action.
2. `src/HurdleVault.sol:176-178` (`DEFICIT_TOLERANCE_BPS`) — a vault that has ever paid a shortfall is closed to new deposits until `deficit` is earned back from excess over par. Excess over par is exactly what a curator in a drawdown rarely produces, so one bad week can close the vault for good (Chaser's deficit is 6.7% of NAV and the replay Chaser carries 9.9% gross). This is the same liveness class as the v1 item 1, moved from the block to the bond history. It also means a vault that survived a loss cannot rebuild AUM, which hurts "real-world usefulness". Make it 10: let the curator clear a deficit by posting bond (a `postBond` that first reduces `deficit`), or let a newcomer pay a pro-rata share of the deficit in kind at entry (`deposit` can mint at a discount to par for the deficit), and add a test for deposit after a shortfall with the curator topping up.
3. README / IDEA / submission — there is still no demo video or recording link (changelog "Not done"). The hackathon rates the demo; the pages tell the story well but the on-chain fire plus keeper park is best shown as a 2–3 min clip. Make it 10: record it (Patient fire → keeper park → Chaser frozen, then a deposit into a fresh vault) and link it in the first five lines of the README.
4. dApp chart units vs README — the README says results are "in USD (same convention as the UI)", but the vault pages state values in mWETH (token1), so after an ETH −9.5% leg the Chaser chart shows depositors and par rising to +5.0% and Patient's par rising about +2.5% while holders lost in USD terms. A first-time reader reads "+5%" as a gain. Make it 10: add a USD toggle (default) using the indexed ETH price already in `Settled` events, or label the axis "vs par, in mWETH" directly on the chart; the headline "Depositors vs par" is right, the strategy and par lines are the ones that mislead.
5. Repo hygiene on the submitted commit — `git status` shows 2 modified and 7 untracked `broadcast/**/run-*.json`, a deleted tracked `app/worker/.probe.mjs`, and README still says `forge test` gives "94 pass … Audit2Regression re-runs the 30 vault tests" while the suite is now 90 pass, 1 skipped with `VaultFixture` (changelog says this was changed). Make it 10: commit or ignore the broadcast files, remove the probe file properly, and fix the README test counts so a judge running `forge test` sees the stated number.
6. Keeper noise on the live pages — Patient's "Keeper runs" lists three `rebalance_park` reverts (23, 18, 26 min ago) and a reverted `settle` ("transaction reverted on chain"), and `/healthz` showed `uptimeSeconds` dropping from 545 to 79 between two calls (worker restarted while I was reviewing). The app is honest about it, which is good, but a judge opening the page sees red lines. Make it 10: explain the reverts in a note (park retries are gated by the strict 30-min price check) or have the keeper skip a doomed call after a `PriceDeviation` simulation, and keep the worker from redeploying near the deadline.
